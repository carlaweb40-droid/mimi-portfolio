About me

Before this trip, I was an educator and storyteller, making Web3 topics simple and selling through Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Then I left Singapore to slow down. For 16 months I've been overlanding, camping, and being welcomed into family homes in Morocco, Mauritania, Senegal, The Gambia and Guinea, plus Lisbon, Porto and France.

It's my first time in Africa and the longest trip of my life. It changed how I see the world, and what I want to do when I come home.

What slow travel taught me

Africa is not one story

North Africa and West Africa are completely different, and both broke the assumptions I came with.

Patience

Electricity cuts out anytime. Internet is a luxury. You learn to wait and adapt.

Strangers become hosts

In every country, someone opened their home to me.

Long trips show you how the world works

Short trips show you places. Staying longer lets you see both sides.
